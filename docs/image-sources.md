The five other non-software capability tiles reuse course-topic images listed above. All capability photos are topic illustrations, not evidence of specific products, personal projects or work samples. Navigation and interface icons come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license) (ISC License).

The education images illustrate course topics. They are not claimed as photographs of Meihan Qian's classes, her universities' facilities, or personal projects. Each school has one link to its official [Northeastern University curriculum](https://sfie.neu.edu.cn/pyfa/list.htm) or [Toulouse mechanical engineering programme](https://fsi.utoulouse.fr/licence-parcours-genie-mecanique-en-aeronautique-gma); course images are not links. University-hosted photos were not copied because the [Toulouse university legal notice](https://aapriss.univ-tlse3.fr/mentions-legales) reserves photographic reproduction rights. Each selected technical image was visually checked to avoid flags, political imagery, and prominent organizational symbols. Local copies are web-sized; course-card display may crop images and slightly desaturate their colors.
